1. The Play page in one minute

  1. Pick an opponent and start. The first game downloads Python for the browser (about 10 MB, once). Big Money is the easy one; the rl-v3 network is the strongest.
  2. Answer at the bottom. The bar at the bottom of the screen always says what you are deciding. To buy, tap a green card in the supply; to play an action or answer an attack, tap a green card in your hand or a button. Treasures are played for you.
  3. Win probability (Settings ⚙, on by default): the advisor network's chance that you win, from your seat, at each of your decisions, with a small graph of the game so far. Watch it move after your buys.
  4. Suggestion (Settings ⚙, off by default): the advisor's favourite answers with its probabilities, a ★ on the board and a gold-edged button. Turn it on to learn, off to test yourself.
  5. Take back (Settings ⚙): undo your last move and try the other one. The opponent is deterministic, so you see exactly what would have changed.
  6. Review: after the game, the moves where the model would have chosen differently, with your win chance at the time.

The page's behaviour (defaults, which network advises, when to ask before ending a turn, the review) is set in one file, play/spec.json; Settings → Game spec shows it.
