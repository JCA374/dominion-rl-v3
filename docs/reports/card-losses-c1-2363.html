Key findings

  1. No card it cannot manage. Against 8 opponents (64,000 games) the worst cards lower its points by 3-4 per 100 games when they are in the kingdom (Library -3.5, Bureaucrat -3.2, Mine -3.0); the best raise them by 7-8 (Sentry, Chapel).
  2. Library, Moat, Smithy and Council Room cost the old champion just as much on the same games: that is the game (slower, terminal-heavy kingdoms shrink a strong player's edge), not a weakness of this model.
  3. The card school worked: on identical games the model gains most over the champion with Chapel (+9.8 points), Village (+5.2), Festival (+3.8), Artisan, Laboratory and Throne Room. Chapel, Village and Throne Room were school cards; it now buys Chapel in 64% of kingdoms (champion 3%).
  4. Where it is weaker than the champion on the same games: Sentry (-5.1) and Gardens (-5.2) - it still gains from both, but less - then Militia (-3.0), Witch (-2.2), Harbinger (-2.1), Workshop (-1.8), Vassal (-1.7). Head to head against the champion, Bureaucrat, Mine, Harbinger and Workshop kingdoms cost 4-5 points.
  5. Against bots it wins 92-97% everywhere; the card effects there are 1-3 points, so the gaps matter mostly against strong nets.
